What the melt is actually tracking

    Progress is the fraction of these that have settled, not a timer. The wax eases toward each step instead of snapping, so a slow entity fetch stalls the drips where a fake timer would sail past it.

    Notes for the build

    One image, not thirty frames. In real melted crayon art the crayons stay glued at the top and only the wax runs, so there is no melt sequence to render. This is a single photo of the finished melt with each drip revealed downward by a mask. Photoreal crayons, photoreal wax, exact progress tracking, and one asset. A loader that needs megabytes of frames before it can show you anything is a loader that needs a loader.

    Unbranded on purpose. The first generation came back as Crayola crayons, logo and all. That is a trademark problem on a commercial product, not a taste one. These wrappers are blank.

    Canvas, not SVG. One canvas redrawn per frame. Your gotchas page has the SVGFollower crash from Chromatica: manual DOM on a timer, unmounted component, insertBefore throw. A loader unmounts the moment loading finishes, which is exactly that trap.

    Real progress, not a timer. registerBootTask(promise) collects the boot promises and progress is settled over total. The wax cannot lie about how loaded the app is.

    Open question: dark mode. The artwork is a white canvas, so on a dark theme this is a bright rectangle. I have left it committed to the light artwork rather than guess at an invert that would wreck the wax colours. Worth a look on your phone in dark mode before we decide.
