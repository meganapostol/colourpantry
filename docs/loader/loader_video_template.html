What the melt is actually tracking

    Progress is the fraction of these that have settled, not a timer. It drives a target position in the video; a proportional controller nudges playbackRate to chase it. The wax never seeks and never lies about how loaded the app is.

    Notes for the build

    The poster is why this works. AJ's rule: ship frame one as an image so the video loads behind it. Here it pays twice, because unmelted crayons are mostly flat canvas and compress to 46 KB, while the finished melt is detail everywhere and cost 154 KB. So the video path has a lighter first paint than the still it replaces, and real wax on top.

    playbackRate, not currentTime. Seeking every frame is janky, worst on iOS Safari, and smooth seeking needs dense keyframes that inflate the file. Instead: err = target - currentTime, then playbackRate = clamp(err * 3, 0.25, 4), and pause when caught up. Loading stalls, the wax freezes mid-drip. Loading jumps, the wax rushes. Scrubbing backwards is the one case that seeks, and that only exists in this mockup.

    iOS autoplay. muted and playsinline are what permit playback at all. Without them it silently never starts.

    720p, delogo'd, retimed. Gemini burns a sparkle watermark into every frame; it is interpolated out with delogo and verified gone across the clip. The source was 10s at 24fps and is retimed to 5s so playbackRate stays in a sane range instead of needing 7x on a fast load.

    Still open: the CDN. AJ's second rule has no answer on Base44 yet. The platform reference is silent on whether public assets are CDN-backed. The poster-frame move works regardless, which is exactly why it goes first.

    Still open: dark mode. The footage is a light canvas, so on a dark theme this is a bright rectangle. Unresolved on purpose rather than guessed at.
